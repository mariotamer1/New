Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

How long does a typical job take?

It depends on the size and scope — a single room is often a day or two, while a full exterior repaint usually runs several days. We'll give you a clear timeline before any work starts, and hold to it.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No — we move and carefully cover furniture, flooring, and fixtures ourselves as part of prep, and put everything back when we're done.

What paint do you use?

We use professional-grade paint suited to the surface and conditions of your job. We're happy to talk through options and finishes when we quote your project.

Can you match HOA-approved or specific colors?

Yes — bring us a swatch, a code, or your HOA's approved palette and we'll match it.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes, no cost and no obligation. Submit a request and we'll follow up with pricing and next steps.

Do you clean up after the job?

Every time. Job sites are left spotless — no leftover tape, drop cloths, or debris.
