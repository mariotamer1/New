Contact Consent

Last updated September 2026

When you check the consent box on our Free Estimate form, here's exactly what you're agreeing to:

What we mean by "contact"

YGM Painting (Ysrael or a member of our team) may reach out to you by phone call, text message, or email — using the phone number and/or email address you entered in the form — to follow up on your estimate request, answer questions, schedule a walkthrough, or discuss your project.

What this consent is for

This is solely so we can respond to the request you submitted. It is not permission for ongoing marketing messages, and we don't enroll you in any mailing list or automated marketing campaign just because you requested an estimate.

What we don't do

  • We don't use automated dialers or mass text-blast services.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.
  • We don't contact you about anything other than the project you asked about, unless you reach out to us again.

Changing your mind

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time — just tell us by phone, text, or email and we'll honor that immediately.

More detail

For the full picture of what we collect and how it's stored, see our Privacy Policy.
