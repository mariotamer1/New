Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This page explains what information YGM Painting collects through this website, why, and how it's handled. We keep this simple and limited to what we actually do — we're a small local painting business, not a data company.

What we collect

When you submit our "Free Estimate" form, or use "Check Quote Status," we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Details you provide about your painting project

We don't use tracking pixels, ad networks, or third-party analytics on this site, and we don't collect any information beyond what you choose to type into our forms.

Why we collect it

Solely to follow up with you about your estimate request — to call, text, or email you back about your project. That's it.

Where it's stored

Submissions are stored in a secured database (provided by Supabase) that only YGM Painting can access, through a password-protected admin login. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests on file so we can reference past conversations if you contact us again. If you'd like your information deleted, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see what information we have on file for you, correct it, or delete it. Just reach out using the contact information on our site.
