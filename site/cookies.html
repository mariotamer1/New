Cookies Policy

Last updated September 2026

Short version: this site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Here's the full picture of what's stored on your device when you visit.

What we don't use

We don't run any advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies on this website. Nothing here follows you to other sites or builds an advertising profile.

What is stored

Your browser may keep a small amount of technical, local information purely to make the site work correctly, such as:

  • Your session staying logged in if you're an admin accessing the requests dashboard
  • Basic technical preferences your browser stores locally (like reduced-motion settings), which never leave your device

None of this identifies you personally or is shared with anyone else, and none of it is used for advertising.

Form submissions

Information you actively type into our "Free Estimate" or "Check Quote Status" forms is sent directly to our database when you submit — that's covered in our Privacy Policy, not by cookies.

Changes

If that ever changes (for example, if we add analytics in the future), we'll update this page to reflect it.
